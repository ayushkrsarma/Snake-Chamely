### Rewards
#### Mukti is used instead of Score aligning with story
<img width="869" height="205" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1f09ea7-5972-49e4-96c4-83550d6b4b25" />

#### Heart 
* Increments +15 Mukti
* Reswapns in every 15 Seconds
* Reswapns randomly at canvas, generally near Cactus

#### Cactus
* Decrement 20 Mukti points
* Appears randomly for permanent position
* Increases speed to uncontrollable levels
  
#### Coin 
* Slows Down Speed to Normal
* Maintains Status Quo, Neither Increment nor Decrement in Score
* Out of 4 times Coin appears at 3 times (3/4) Chances of Apperances
  
#### Ruby 
* Not Directly Increments in Score
* While it generates 4 Hearts
